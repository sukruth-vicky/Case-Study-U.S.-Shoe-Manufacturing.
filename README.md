U.S. Shoe Manufacturing – Case Study (McKinsey Style)

Overview
This project analyzes whether a U.S. shoe manufacturer should offshore production. It compares profitability per unit, total landed cost, and the impact of lead times on sourcing decisions.

Unit Profitability
- U.S.: $10/unit
- Vietnam: $15/unit
- Vietnam is 50% more profitable due to lower COGS and labor.

Lead Time
- U.S.: 1 month
- Vietnam: 3 months
- Longer lead time → higher working capital cost.

Offshoring Formula
Fraction Offshored = 1 - (δh) / (2μΔc)

Where:  
- δ = demand volatility (20)  
- μ = average demand (1000)  
- h = holding cost ($1)  
- Δc = Vietnam cost advantage ($5)  

Result: 80% Vietnam, 20% U.S.

Recommendation
- Manufacture 80% in Vietnam for cost advantage.
- Retain 20% in U.S. for flexibility and quality control.
- Run sensitivity analysis on tariffs, transport, and defect rates.
